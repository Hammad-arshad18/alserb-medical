# Hero image prompts

The site hero displays a 4:3 image at roughly 1600 × 1200. It sits on a deep navy background, so the image should have dark edges, a clear focal point, and no text or brand markings.

## Recommended hero prompt

> Editorial photograph for a premium medical equipment supplier: a close-up of a modern surgical light and operating microscope inside a quiet operating theatre. Deep navy and teal colour grade, soft cinematic lighting, bright white light head as the focal point, precise stainless steel, matte white equipment surfaces, subtle blue reflections, shallow depth of field, calm and sterile atmosphere, high-end architectural photography, medium format realism, ultra-detailed, no people, no patients, no text, no logos, no brand markings, 4:3 aspect ratio.

**Negative prompt**

> text, letters, watermark, logo, brand name, people, patient, surgery in progress, blood, gore, cluttered room, visible cables everywhere, overexposed, flat lighting, green colour cast, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, CGI, low resolution, distorted equipment, duplicate objects, cropped light head.

## Alternative: wider operating theatre

> Wide editorial photograph of a modern operating theatre, empty and prepared for use, surgical table and ceiling-mounted surgical lights, patient monitor and anaesthesia equipment at the edges, deep navy and cyan colour grade, soft directional light, clean stainless steel and white surfaces, realistic architectural photography, calm and premium, no people, no text, no logos, 4:3.

## Alternative: equipment still life

> Premium product photograph of a modern patient monitor on a stand in a minimal clinical room, bright screen with blue and green vital-sign traces, soft daylight from the left, white walls, subtle blue shadows, shallow depth of field, realistic medical equipment photography, no people, no text, no logos, 4:3.

## Art direction notes

- Keep the edges dark enough to blend into the navy hero background.
- Leave the bright focal point near the centre-right of the frame.
- Avoid patient faces, visible blood, or an active procedure.
- Do not include manufacturer logos or model numbers.
- Use a 4:3 crop, then resize to 1600 × 1200 and save as a progressive JPEG.
- Keep the image sharp around the equipment and softly blurred in the background.
